Music

Plastic Heart Surgery is an Experimental Electronic music producer and DJ residing in Toronto. She is not (yet) known for the phattest beats you've ever heard that will make you twerk like you're on horse tranquilizer and Diet Coke®️. She is also known for her impressively long songs that end up boring you if you're a headphone user because tbh you need that booty rattling on a thicc soundsystem that'll make your bottom shake uncontrollably. I didn't know how to twerk to a 5/4 beat until today - testimonial from a first time listener that somehow learned what a time signature was from ancient knowledge delivered through sound.
